# Track 08 — Continuous Practice & Career

Purpose: ongoing, not a terminal stage — revisit this track's habits regularly rather than completing it once.

### Staying Current via Disclosed Reports
- Purpose: reading others' methodology is one of the highest-leverage ongoing activities in bounty hunting
- Primary resource: HackerOne Hacktivity, Bugcrowd/Intigriti blogs (see `resources.md` and `social_media.md`)
- Practice: read and summarize (in your own words, per copyright practice) one disclosed report weekly
- Status: not-started

### Community Engagement
- Purpose: the community is where new techniques and tool releases surface first, often before formal write-ups
- Primary resource: the creator list in `social_media.md`
- Practice: follow the recommended daily/weekly/monthly cadence already defined there — don't duplicate that logic here
- Status: not-started

### Live Hacking Events & Career Progression
- Purpose: longer-horizon goals — live events, invite-only programs, and eventually career transition
- Primary resource: platform announcements for live-hacking event eligibility criteria
- Practice: track your own progress against a chosen platform's live-event eligibility bar
- Status: not-started
