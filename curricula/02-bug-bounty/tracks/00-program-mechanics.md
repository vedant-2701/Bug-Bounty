# Track 00 — Program Mechanics, Scope & Legal

Purpose: this is not optional context — it's the difference between legitimate research and unauthorized access. Do this before touching a real program.

### Scope & Rules of Engagement 🔑
**Purpose:** the single most common way to get banned from a platform is misreading scope, not finding too few bugs.
**Prerequisites:** none
**Core concept:** in-scope vs out-of-scope assets, prohibited techniques (often: automated scanning without rate limits, social engineering, physical testing, DoS), what "safe harbor" actually promises and doesn't.
**Primary resource:** read 5 real program policies end-to-end on HackerOne/Bugcrowd before hunting anywhere
**Secondary resources:** disclose.io safe harbor project
**Practice:** for one chosen program, write down its scope boundaries and prohibited techniques from memory, then verify against the policy
**Real-world example:** search for public incidents of researchers testing out-of-scope assets and the consequences
**Cross-links:** none — foundational, gatekeeping content
**Expected outcome:** can correctly classify an asset as in/out of scope for a given program without re-reading the policy every time
