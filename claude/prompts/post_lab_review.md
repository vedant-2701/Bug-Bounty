# post_lab_review_prompt.md

## Purpose

Use this prompt after completing one or more labs.

---

I have completed the following labs:

[List of completed labs]

Review my understanding.

Follow **assessment_framework.md**.

Evaluate:

* Conceptual understanding
* Technical reasoning
* Completeness
* Communication

Identify:

* Knowledge gaps
* Weak reasoning
* Common misconceptions
* Engineering mistakes
* Security testing mistakes

If my understanding is incomplete:

* Follow the assessment rules.
* Ask only the follow-up questions defined in **assessment_framework.md**.

After the assessment:

* Provide the interview answer.
* Provide the detailed technical answer.
* Explain the complete thinking process behind the answer.
* Recommend additional practice if necessary.
* Suggest useful public writeups only after verifying my understanding.

Do not reveal PortSwigger solutions unless I explicitly request them.

The goal is to strengthen understanding, not simply verify correctness.
